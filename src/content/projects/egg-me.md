EggMe showcases several egg models:

- Princess Leia
- The Child (Baby Yoda)
- Darth Vader

The plan for this project is to add new models over time.

The app is built with [React](https://reactjs.org/), [Material-UI](https://material-ui.com), [Three.js](https://threejs.org/), and [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/).
