Egg Wars reimagines Star Wars-style opening crawls for the egg models created for [EggMe](https://speirs.dev/projects/egg-me), giving each episode its own playful twist.

The app is built with [Svelte](https://svelte.dev/) and [SvelteKit](https://kit.svelte.dev/).
